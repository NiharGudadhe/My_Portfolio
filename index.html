ABOUT ME

I am a Data Scientist & Analyst based in Nagpur, India, with 2 years 9 months of hands-on experience taking problems from raw data to deployed models — EDA, feature engineering, model building, evaluation, and deployment across regression, classification, NLP, and anomaly detection.

At Influx Control Panel, I built and deployed a Random Forest pricing model that cut quotation turnaround from days to seconds, set up automated ELT pipelines (MySQL, dbt, PostgreSQL), and delivered Power BI dashboards behind a 10% lift in sales conversion, a 12% rise in shop-floor throughput, and a 14% drop in product returns. I also integrated CRM and ERP data to streamline the Quote-to-Cash process.

Outside work, I build end-to-end projects: a complaint triage platform on 150,000 CFPB complaints with fine-tuned RoBERTa, a local RAG question-answering system over 49,877 Amazon reviews, and a live shopper purchase-intent MLOps pipeline with MLflow and drift monitoring. I work in Python, SQL, Docker, and CI/CD, and I document assumptions and limitations so results can be trusted.

My goal is to keep building production-grade ML systems that turn raw business data into measurable operational impact.
