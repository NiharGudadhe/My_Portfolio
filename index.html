ABOUT ME

I am a Data Scientist based in Nagpur, India, with hands-on experience across the full data science lifecycle — EDA, feature engineering, model building, and evaluation for prediction, classification, and time series forecasting problems.

I'm comfortable working in Python and SQL, with a solid grounding in statistics and a track record of turning messy business problems into working ML solutions, most notably integrating CRM and ERP systems to streamline the Quote-to-Cash process.

I'm also exploring Generative AI and LLMs (prompt engineering, RAG) alongside my core ML and analytics work in Power BI.

My goal is to keep building production-grade ML systems that turn raw business data into measurable operational impact.
